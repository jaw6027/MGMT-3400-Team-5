How these numbers are calculated

Totals include all records, including any labeled Demo. Only Paid work completed counts as a paid job, and only those records’ paid amounts count as revenue. Overall referral cost per paid job = all partners’ recorded costs ÷ completed paid jobs; “—” means no paid jobs. Costs are lifetime totals, not filtered by date. Revenue is not profit: labor, materials, taxes, and overhead are not included.

MGMT 3400 · ENTREPRENEURSHIP

Finding customers, one introduction at a time.

CraftNDetail is a group venture for clothing alterations, repairs, and custom clothing. This tracker supports Priority 2: figure out who our customers will be by organizing possible retailer referral relationships and following their outcomes.

The idea: boutiques, thrift and vintage shops, dry cleaners, formalwear shops, and other local businesses might introduce customers who need a better fit or a second life for their clothes.

What we can learn

In a future pilot, compare referrals, completed paid jobs, revenue, and referral cost per paid job. Explore which services people request and where leads stop progressing. These are learning signals, not proof of demand or profitability.

Classroom prototype, fictional examples

Every initial retailer and customer is invented. Demo records are labeled throughout the app and in exports. We have not verified customer demand or conducted research through this tool.

Your data stays here.

Records are saved in this browser’s local storage on this device. They do not sync with classmates, other browsers, or other devices. No analytics, external fonts, or tracking services are used. The app sends no data and contacts no one.

Local storage is not encrypted by this app. Anyone with access to this browser profile may see the records. Clearing browser data can erase them. CSV exports contain contact information if entered; handle them carefully. CSV is an export, not a restorable backup.

Consent is a workflow reminder

Each referral requires confirmation that the customer agreed to be contacted. The checkbox records your confirmation; it does not verify legal consent. Enter contact details only after agreement. For class, use aliases and invented details.
